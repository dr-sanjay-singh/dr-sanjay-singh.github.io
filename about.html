Building chiral molecules with light and organic catalysts for drug discovery

Dr. Sanjay Singh is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India. His research focuses on drug discovery and sustainable organic synthesis.

He received his Ph.D. in Organic Chemistry from the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi under the supervision of Prof. Ravi P. Singh, where he worked on asymmetric organocatalysis and vinylogous reactivity. He subsequently carried out postdoctoral research on photoredox and energy-transfer catalysis with Prof. Sungwoo Hong at the IBS Center for Catalytic Hydrocarbon Functionalizations (CCHF), KAIST, South Korea.

His work has appeared in journals including Science Advances, Nature Communications, Green Chemistry, The Journal of Organic Chemistry and Chemistry: A European Journal, and he is an inventor on two Indian patent applications.

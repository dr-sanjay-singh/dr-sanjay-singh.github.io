Top  /  Research

Organic synthesis through photocatalysis, organocatalysis and sustainable methods

Photocatalysis

Light-Driven Bond Construction

Visible-light photoredox, ligand-to-metal charge transfer (LMCT) and triplet energy-transfer catalysis provide mild routes to strained, three-dimensional frameworks. Recent work includes a visible-light-mediated strain-release [2π + 2σ] cycloaddition that delivers oxa-spiro bicyclohexanes with high stereocontrol.

Organocatalysis

Asymmetric Organocatalysis

Small chiral organic catalysts, including thioureas, squaramides, peptide catalysts and chiral phosphoric acids, are used to build stereocentres in C–C and C–X bond formation. Vinylogous reactivity of coumarins and oxindoles and desymmetrizing annulations give access to chiral pentafulvenes and spirocycles.

Drug Discovery

Scaffolds for Medicinal Chemistry

Stereoselective syntheses of medicinally relevant scaffolds such as α-carboline alkaloids, functionalized coumarins and oxindoles, with an eye on bioactivity and bioimaging applications of the new chiral molecules.

Sustainable Synthesis

Greener Catalytic Methods

Metal-free catalysis, recyclable homogeneous catalytic systems and efficient C–H functionalization strategies that reduce waste and step count in the synthesis of complex organic molecules.
